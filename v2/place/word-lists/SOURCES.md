# Our Word lists

- `answers-en.txt`, `answers-ar.txt`: hand-picked everyday words used as the daily word (4–7 letters).
- `dict-en.txt`: guess dictionary from `an-array-of-english-words` 2.0.0 (MIT), filtered to a–z, 4–7 letters.
- `dict-ar.txt`: guess dictionary from hermitdave/FrequencyWords `ar_50k` (OpenSubtitles 2018, CC BY-SA 4.0), normalized (no diacritics; أ إ آ → ا, ة → ه, ى → ي), 4–7 letters, plus every Arabic answer word.
