How to play

Start with 8 cards. Match the color, number or symbol, or play a Wild. Empty your hand to win. No call-out is needed when one card remains.

Skip and Reverse both skip the other player in this two-player version. The multicolored 8 is Wild: choose any color, not draw 8. +2 and +4 penalties stack, regardless of color: you may accept the full penalty instead of countering. If you cannot counter, it is drawn automatically. Shield reflects that total back and chooses a color; a Shield can reflect another Shield. The eye card is X-Ray: its power activates when played, not while held. Choose a color and privately reveal one random opponent card until it leaves their hand.

Dead Red makes every player without red draw 2. Boo Blue makes the player with the fewest cards draw 2; ties affect both. These two effects cannot be shielded. Their effects resolve before checking for a winner, so they can make their owner draw too. A final +2 or +4 ends the game immediately when the attacker empties their hand; no further counter or draw is needed. Each completed win adds one win, without card-value scoring.

Our two-player rules: drawing is locked when you have a playable card (except accepting a pending penalty). Draw one card when you have no match. If it fits, play that drawn card. When no counter is available, draw penalties are taken immediately and automatically, ending your turn. An empty draw pile is reshuffled from earlier discards. Two turns with no draw or move end in a tie. The deck contains the standard 108 cards plus two each of Shield, X-Ray, Dead Red and Boo Blue.

Each move and result is saved. Minimize or switch tabs to pause; resume when ready. Both players must release their own pause. Leave game ends play without awarding a win. Timer choices and these detailed two-player rules are for Our Place, not a claim of exact Plato behavior.

How to play

Each player gets 7 tiles from a double-six set. Match a number at either end. If you cannot play, draw one tile at a time until you can. Pass only when the draw pile is empty and you have no match.

Empty your hand to win the opponent’s remaining pips. If both players pass, the lower pip total wins the difference; equal totals are a draw. Starting player alternates each round. Reach the chosen 50 or 100 points to win the match. Match wins and the latest 100 results are saved; leaving early does not count as a win. Solo difficulty changes how the computer chooses from its own tiles.

Your game saves after every move. Close the panel or visit chat to pause play. Resume game continues from the saved turn; Leave game ends the match.
