Privacy Policy

Updated September 29, 2026

Our Place is a private, invitation-only space for its authorized users to chat, share media, plan activities, and use shared features.

Information used by the app

The app processes account details, messages, uploaded media, posts, reactions, shared activity records, preferences, and connection information to provide its features. Shared content is available to the other authorized participant. Authentication cookies maintain signed-in sessions. If notifications are enabled, push subscription details are stored to deliver alerts.

Services and AI features

Render hosts the app and its stored content. Supabase provides account authentication. AI features use OpenAI and may send the request and relevant app context to generate a response. Media features may contact services such as YouTube. These providers process requests under their own policies. Private app content is not published by these policy pages.

Google Drive backups

Connecting Google Drive is optional and available to the app administrator. Our Place requests the drive.file permission, which covers files created by the app or specifically shared with it, rather than unrestricted access to your Drive.

The current backup feature creates encrypted copies of app data in the connected Google Drive account and checks the uploaded file size and checksum. It stores an encrypted authorization token on the app server to upload future backups without repeated sign-in. Backup status and upload times are also stored. Google Drive authorization and backup data are used for backup and recovery, not advertising or AI model training.

Storage and deletion

App data remains stored until removed through available app controls or by the administrator. Deleting live content does not remove it from existing backup archives. Local backup rotation and separately retained Drive archives have different retention periods. Drive archives remain until the account owner deletes them; they count toward that account's storage allowance.

You can revoke Google Drive access in your Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking access stops future authorized uploads but does not delete existing archives. Delete those files separately in Drive if desired. Contact the app administrator through your existing private contact channel for account, data export, deletion, or privacy requests.

Security

Access to app data requires an authorized account. Backup archives and stored Google refresh tokens are encrypted. Recovery requires the corresponding recovery secret, which should be kept securely and separately. These measures do not guarantee against every security incident or data loss.
